Privacy Policy for Pop Calc

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pop Calc ("the app," "we," "us") handles information. This policy applies only to Pop Calc and covers how the app collects, uses, and shares data.

Summary

Pop Calc does not collect, store, or transmit any personal or sensitive user data. The app has no account system, no user profiles, and no internet connection requirement for its core calculator features. All calculations and your calculation history stay on your device.

Information we collect

None. Pop Calc does not collect:

  • Personal information (name, email address, phone number)
  • Precise or approximate location
  • Contacts, photos, files, or other device content
  • Device identifiers, advertising ID, or analytics identifiers
  • Usage analytics or crash reports

The app does not include any advertising SDK and does not display ads.

Information stored on your device

The following data is created and stored locally on your device only. It is never sent to us or to any third party, and we have no access to it:

DataPurposeWhere it's stored
Calculation historyLets you review and reuse past calculationsLocal app storage on your device
App settings (theme, sound, haptics)Remembers your preferencesLocal app storage on your device

Uninstalling the app permanently deletes this data. We do not back it up to any server we operate.

In-app purchases

Pop Calc offers an optional one-time "Pro" purchase for additional themes and features. Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment details (such as card numbers). Google's handling of payment information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. We only receive confirmation from Google Play that a purchase was made, which is used solely to unlock Pro features on your device.

Permissions the app uses

PermissionWhy it's needed
VibrationProvides haptic feedback when you tap keys
BillingEnables the optional in-app Pro purchase through Google Play

Pop Calc does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, storage, or any other sensitive device permission.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any user data with third parties, because we do not collect any user data in the first place. The only third party involved in the app at all is Google Play Billing, which processes optional purchases as described above.

Children's privacy

Pop Calc is not directed at children and is not designed to appeal primarily to children. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because the app collects no data at all.

Data retention and deletion

Because all data stays on your device, you are always in control of it. To delete all data created by the app, uninstall it, or clear the app's storage from your device's system settings.

Security

Since no data leaves your device, there is no server-side data of yours to secure. Standard Android app sandboxing protects the app's local storage from other apps on your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example, if a future version of the app adds a new feature that uses data differently — this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change. Continued use of the app after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
