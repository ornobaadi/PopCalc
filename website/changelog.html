What's new

Every release since the first, newest first. 9 versions and counting.

v1.6.0Build 17LATESTCurrency converter, the Dunesday bundle, sharper maths
  • Currency converter: 33 currencies, right at the front of the converter. It works offline with rates built into the app, and one tap fetches today's rates from the internet when you want them. PopCalc stays fully offline otherwise
  • Two limited-edition skins until 11 January 2027: Mirage (living dunes under a sky that follows your clock) and Reckoning (a doomsday clock on your phone's own time), each with film-style sounds, haptics and a hidden scene. A first-time hint shows you where to hold
  • The Dunesday bundle: both drops together for less, right at the top of Settings
  • Sharper maths: percentages add up properly (1% − 1% = 0), and square and cube roots are exact (√2 × √2 = 2)
  • Updates in the app: PopCalc tells you when a new version is on Google Play and installs it in the background
  • PopCalc in Quick Settings: open the calculator from your pull-down panel
  • Data units, made clear: GB vs GiB side by side, plus TiB and Mbit/Gbit
  • Tiny answers no longer show as 0, and huge ones round properly
  • Tidier Settings: app icons moved further down; Obsidian, Synthwave, Clay and Candy retired
v1.5.1Build 15Fixes, and a joke that's too big
  • No more freezing on big powers: calculations like compound interest, (1 + 0.055 ÷ 365)^365, used to freeze the app. They now answer instantly. Thanks to the user who reported it
  • Editing a number is easier: tap a number in your calculation and backspace now removes one digit at a time, so you can fix 9999 into 9990 without retyping it all
  • Editing can't break your maths anymore: typing digits while an operator like × is selected no longer turns it into a stray number
  • Results too big to show now come with a new set of jokes. That's what she said
  • The case file has a new lead: one of the editor's riddles is new. If you were partway through, you keep your progress
  • Rate PopCalc from Settings, if you feel like it. One person makes this app, and a review helps others find it

  • Pick your app icon: eight icons to choose from in Settings, free for everyone. The new icon shows on your home screen as soon as you leave the app, no restart
  • A launch that matches: PopCalc now opens on the icon you chose and settles smoothly into the app, with no flash of the old logo
  • The ??? skin is now a case to crack. Tap it in Settings and the editor hands you a confidential case file: five one-line riddles, each hiding somewhere in the app. Crack them all and the secret skin arrives in a spill of ink

  • Quieter in the background: PopCalc no longer shows up in your phone's volume controls while you're not using it
  • Fits better on small screens and edge-to-edge displays
  • Clearer wording in Settings

  • Your skins come first: anything you have bought moves up beside the free skins in Settings, and steps aside under "Yours" in the store
