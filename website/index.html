05 / Questions

FAQ

Is PopCalc free?

Yes. The calculator, scientific mode, unit and currency converter, three skins and all app icons are free. Premium skins and materials are optional one-time purchases.

Does it show ads or collect data?

No ads, no account and no data collected. PopCalc works fully offline; it only goes online if you ask for live currency rates.

Is it on iPhone?

Not yet. PopCalc is Android only for now.

Will it slow down my phone?

No. If you want it even lighter, Lite Effects Mode draws flat numerals with no 3D or tilt.

I bought a skin. Do I get it on my new phone?

Yes. Purchases belong to your Google account. Open the skin store and tap Restore Purchases.
